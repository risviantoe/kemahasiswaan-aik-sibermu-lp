## Users
Proposed primary audience: new and active Universitas Siber Muhammadiyah students seeking communities, AIK learning, activities, achievements, and support. Competition judges evaluate the result.

## Product Purpose
One-page Kemahasiswaan and Al-Islam dan Kemuhammadiyahan prototype for the 2026 SiberMu landing-page competition. Concept proposed and carried into the authorized prototype: Ruang Tumbuh SiberMu — Terhubung, Bertumbuh, Berkontribusi.

The current deliverable is a locally reviewable prototype. Submission preparation/publication is a later stage. Deadline from supplied brief: 30 September 2026.

## Product Principles
- Combine useful student tasks with substantive AIK content.
- Make destinations and activity status clear.
- Label missing evidence; never manufacture institutional claims.
- Keep the first implementation small enough to review directly in a browser.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Semantic headings, visible focus, reduced-motion support, accessible mobile navigation, sufficient contrast, and no dependency on hovering for core content.
